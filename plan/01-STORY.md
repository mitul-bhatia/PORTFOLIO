## The problem Version 1 named

Every personal portfolio in this cohort looks the same. The old look — warm parchment, Fraunces, burnt sienna, 0px corners, notebook framing — was distinctive eighteen months ago. It is now a template family.

Version 1’s fix: **do not change the palette; change the interaction texture** so a templated site cannot fake it.

Version 2’s expansion: that texture has to live inside a **story** and a **modular site**, not only a 400vh single-page scroll. Recruiters and studio leads do not “experience a GSAP hero.” They ask: *who is this, can they ship, can I verify it in two minutes, can I ask a follow-up without emailing.*

---

## The story in one sentence

**Mitul Bhatia designs agentic systems the way an engineer designs a plant: explicit state, measured latency, and a diagram you can audit — not a vibe.**

Everything on the site is evidence for that sentence. If a module does not serve it, it does not ship.

## Three acts (visitor journey)

### Act I — Orientation (Home, first viewport + next two modules)

The visitor should be able to answer, without scrolling into a dump of skills:

- Name and role: **Mitul Bhatia — AI Agent & Systems Engineer**
- Institution stamp: Newton School of Technology, Class of 2028
- The three pillars as *proof-bearing* cards, not adjectives
- One flagship (AEGIS) visible as the “start here” case

Motion in this act: kinetic name, then **one** second type treatment on the tagline. No extra flourishes.

### Act II — Proof (Work index → case studies)

The visitor should be able to verify:

- AEGIS, Flately, Agrovers as flagships with real metrics and 5-node pipelines
- Secondary work as a denser index (CreditSense, Loan classifier, F1, VULNSWARM)
- Schematics that **draw themselves** (Anime.js) — this is the signature that templates cannot copy without understanding the pipelines
- Metrics that **count to real numbers** (Sliding Number pattern)

### Act III — Person + conversation (About, Contact, AI concierge)

The visitor should leave knowing:

- Methodology (four points) and academic / CP signal (9.80, 1400+ LC, 900+ CF, AI Studio GPU)
- How to hire or write: email, resume, GitHub, LinkedIn
- That they can **ask the site** grounded questions and be walked to the right page

The bubbly bot is Act III made available from every page as a persistent module. It does not replace the story; it is a librarian for the story.

## Tone of voice

- Engineering-notebook, first person, no startup poetry.
- Metrics are exact (`<24ms`, `99.8%`). Never “blazing fast.”
- Pillars keep their proof points from `mydata.md` verbatim.
- The bot speaks like a concise lab assistant, not a mascot with a name that tries to be cute in copy. Visual: bubbly. Voice: precise.

Banned on-site language: “passionate,” “synergy,” “cutting-edge,” “full-stack ninja,” fake testimonials, invented internships.

---

## What “modular aesthetic” means here

Not a dashboard of random widgets. Modular means:

1. **Shared shells** — page frame, masthead, footer, paper texture, cursor, bot dock — identical on every route.
2. **Composable proof modules** — MetricStrip, PipelineSchematic, PillarCard, SkillCluster, CaseHero — drop onto Home or a case study without restyling.
3. **One signature interaction per surface** — Home: kinetic type; Case study: schematic draw-in; About: portrait + methodology; Bot: morphing panel.

Pretty = paper stock, type, alignment, and motion restraint. Pretty is **not** more color, blobs, or glass.

## Competitive contrast (Manus research job, later)

When Manus (or a human) audits other AI/ML student sites, score them on:

- Can you name their flagship metric after 15 seconds?
- Do diagrams encode a real pipeline or a generic “AI brain” illustration?
- Is the about page a resume dump or a methodology?
- Does anything on the site require understanding their own systems to implement?

This site wins on the last two. Do not chase their palettes.
